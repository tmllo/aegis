# Contract changelog

Any change to the shape of `storm_forecast.json` or `crew_data.json` goes here first, with date, who, what, and why. Tell the other person before merging.

- 2026-11-14 18:15: v1 created (starter kit).
- 2026-11-15: Crew scoring rule, for Emile's live check-in. The keys in `crew_data.json` are unchanged. A check-in is scored on the mean of that value and the previous 2 days, against the personal baseline (mean and sd of the first 14 days). Amber when the relevant side of z reaches 2. Red when it reaches 3, or when the raw value is outside `absolute_red`. Sleep, mood and exercise stay low-side only. Heart rate, blood pressure, glucose and temperature stay two-sided. Daily dose, cumulative dose and bone loss stay on their absolute limits and are not averaged. The full rule is the client-side section of `DATA_CONTRACT.md`. The simulated storm dose in the crew series moves from 10 Nov 2026 to 5 Sep 2026. That is a data change, not a schema change.
- 2026-11-15: New file `web/public/data/real_crew.json`. Inspiration4 CBC and chemistry (NASA OSDR OSD-575, crew C001–C004), scored against each person's pre-flight mean. Does not change `storm_forecast.json` or `crew_data.json`, and it is not an input to the check-in scorer. `far_from_baseline` means a post-flight value inside the printed lab range and at least 2 pre-flight sample SD from that person's mean. R+45 glucose under 50 mg/dL and R+45 potassium over 5.5 mmol/L are sample-handling artefacts. The C003 L-92 CBC row is excluded (shifted columns). Shape is the `real_crew.json` section of `DATA_CONTRACT.md`.

Details and reasons in `docs/backend/BACKEND-PLAN.md` section 4. Nothing changes until Emile agrees.

## PROPOSED 2026-11-15 22:45 (Emile's app, simplicity pass): two daily metrics the app cannot show yet
- `hrv_ms` (heart rate variability, morning RMSSD, ms, two-sided, region `heart`): a recovery and stress signal the crew wants next to resting heart rate. Not in `crew_data.json`; the app shows nothing for it until it lands. Suggested: one value per day in `series`, baseline like `hr`.
- `weight_kg` (body weight, weekly, low-side and high-side, region `whole_body`): tracks nutrition and fluid loss on long missions. Not in `crew_data.json`; the app shows nothing for it until it lands. Suggested: one value per week in `series` (other days null), scored on absolute change from the first week.
- Both are additive keys in `metrics` and `series`; no existing key changes. Tomas's lane: nothing in the app changes until they are in the file.
